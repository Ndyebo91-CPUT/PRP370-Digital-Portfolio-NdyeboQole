## Reflection: STAR Technique (Identifying Skills and Interests for ICT Job Applications)

**Situation:** In order to improve my application for ICT jobs, I had to determine my interests and talents amidst a competitive job market where technical proficiency and self-awareness are highly valued. This need became clear as I prepared for the Project Presentation 3 (PRP370S) assessment, recognizing that aligning my skills with industry demands could set me apart.

**Task:** The task was to determine my key skills and interests through a structured workshop and apply them to my career planning, ensuring I could present a compelling case to potential employers in the ICT sector, such as roles in software development or network administration.

**Action:** On February 12, 2025, I participated in a career workshop organized by the university, engaging in a skills evaluation exercise that included a self-assessment questionnaire and group activities. The evaluation highlighted my strengths in Java programming, problem-solving, and attention to detail. I took thorough notes on how these skills matched ICT employment opportunities, focusing on roles requiring coding and system optimization. Afterward, I practiced drafting a resume, incorporating a "Skills" section with Java proficiency, a "Projects" section featuring my PRP370 digital portfolio, and a summary emphasizing my technical growth, refining it based on feedback from workshop facilitators.
Result: This process boosted my confidence in my abilities, particularly in Java programming, allowing me to create a plan to enhance these skills through personal projects like developing a small app. I improved my CV by highlighting these strengths with specific examples, such as debugging code to improve application performance, which I plan to further tailor for job applications in the next semester.
